### `>_` ABOUT ME

I am an **Artificial Intelligence & Data Science** undergraduate at **Vidyavardhini's College of Engineering & Technology (VCET), Vasai Road (Mumbai Metropolitan Region)**, originally from **Kolhapur, Maharashtra**. I specialize in building full-stack web architectures, secure enterprise banking systems, AI-powered platforms, and embedded hardware systems from the ground up.

- 🔭 **Current Flagship Build:** Architecting **Atomic AI** — next-generation intelligent workflow & LLM integration suite
- 🎓 **Academic Track:** B.E. in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science @ **VCET** *(Div G • Batch G1)*
- 🧠 **Core Engineering Foundations:** Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA), Computer Organization & Architecture (COA), ACID Database Transactions & Object-Oriented Programming
- 🖥️ **Hardware & Systems Architecture:** Embedded Microcontroller Prototyping (Arduino Uno) & Desktop PC Hardware Architecture (Micro-ATX Systems, PSU & Component Diagnostics)
- ⚡ **Engineering Philosophy:** *"I turn my crazy ideas into real-world engineering projects!"*
